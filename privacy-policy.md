# Privacy Policy — Omnix

**Effective date:** September 4, 2026

Omnix ("the app") is a universal Wi‑Fi TV remote for Roku, LG webOS, Samsung Tizen, Android TV, and VIDAA televisions, published by Quahhar ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## Summary

- The app has **no user accounts** and we run **no servers**. Your remote-control traffic goes directly from your phone to your TV over your local Wi‑Fi network.
- We do **not** collect, store, or sell your personal data.
- The free version shows ads through **Google AdMob**, which collects limited device data to serve ads (details below). Buying **Omnix Pro** removes ads.

## Information the app uses on your device

| Data | Purpose | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Local network info (Wi‑Fi subnet, mDNS/SSDP discovery) | Find TVs on your network | Stays between your phone and your TV |
| TV pairing credentials (tokens, keys, certificates) | Reconnect to TVs you have paired | Stored only on your device |
| Saved device list & app settings | Remember your TVs and preferences | Stored only on your device |
| Microphone / speech recognition (optional) | The trackpad's "type to send" dictation converts your speech to text and sends that text to your TV | Speech is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition; we never receive it |

You can use the app without granting the microphone permission; dictation is the only feature that needs it.

## Advertising (free version)

The free version uses **Google AdMob** to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, coarse location derived from IP, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses ad data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

- In the EEA/UK, the app shows a **consent form** (Google User Messaging Platform) before serving personalized ads; you may decline and receive non-personalized ads.
- On iOS, the app respects **App Tracking Transparency**: if you decline tracking, ads are non-personalized and the app keeps working fully.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

Purchasing **Omnix Pro** removes all ads.

## In-app purchases

**Omnix Pro** is sold as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time lifetime unlock. Subscriptions renew through your store account until you cancel; you can manage or cancel one at any time from **Settings → Manage subscription**, or in your App Store / Google Play account settings.

Payments are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play — we never see your payment details.

To keep your **Omnix Pro** unlock working across reinstalls and devices, purchases are validated through **RevenueCat**, a third-party purchase-infrastructure provider acting on our behalf. RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt, your device platform and app version, and a randomly generated anonymous app user ID. That ID is not linked to your name, email, or any account — the app has no accounts. RevenueCat does not receive your payment details. See [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

Your entitlement is also mirrored on your device so the app knows you own Pro while offline.

## Children

The app is a TV remote intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data retention & deletion

All app data (paired TVs, settings) lives on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We keep no servers and no user accounts of our own. Ad-related data is governed by Google’s policies linked above; purchase records are retained by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their own policies, and you can ask us to delete a RevenueCat record using the contact address below.
